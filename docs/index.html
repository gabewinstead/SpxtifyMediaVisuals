Why people keep it open

Small footprint. Big atmosphere.

Keep your music close without opening another full-size player. Choose the mood, the layout, and how much of the song you want to see.

🎤 Synchronized lyrics

Follow the song in real time with lyrics fetched from LRCLIB — no API key required.

🎨 Album-art color

Artwork brings its own accent color, blur, and atmosphere to the overlay.

🖥️ Always on top

Frameless, draggable, resizable, and ready to sit beside your work or over a game.

▦ Three views

Switch between focused lyrics, album cover, and a combined now-playing canvas.

⚡ Quietly automatic

Reads Windows SMTC or Linux MPRIS and updates playback as your music changes.

↻ Automatic updates

Signed releases check for updates and install them without making you hunt for a new build.

Try it

Make your desktop sound like you.

Start with the latest release, then tune the colors, type, blur, opacity, and layout from Settings.
